Jobs to be done

"When I come across a long podcast episode, I want to quickly tell if it's worth my time and jump to the parts I care about, so I don't have to gamble an hour to find out."

Kano

Delighter. Nobody asks for chapters on a podcast today, so absence costs nothing — but presence changes how the catalogue feels to use.

RICE

Reach high (podcast browsing is near-universal), impact high, confidence medium-high on existing transcription work, effort medium at catalogue scale.

Riskiest assumption

That listeners trust an AI chapter boundary enough to skip on it. Test first on a 5% cohort of shows over 60 minutes, measuring completion, not clicks.
